**This is placeholder content — replace it with your own disclaimer before publishing your site.**

Requirements for a disclaimer page vary a lot by country and by what your site actually does (comments, ads, affiliate links, editorial content, etc.), so there's no generic text that would be correct for everyone. If you're not sure what you need, talk to a lawyer in your jurisdiction rather than reusing someone else's disclaimer verbatim.

A disclaimer page commonly covers things like:

- Who runs the site and how to contact them
- Whether the content is personal opinion, professional advice, or something else
- How comments (if enabled) are moderated
- The license under which your content is published (see [LICENSE](https://github.com/) in the repository for the theme's own license)
